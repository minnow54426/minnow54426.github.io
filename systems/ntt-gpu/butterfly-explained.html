1. NTT and butterflies

The number-theoretic transform (NTT) evaluates a polynomial at special points. All calculations wrap around a prime, so the results stay exact. To multiply two polynomials, transform both, multiply the matching evaluations, then run the inverse transform.

A butterfly updates two values. Multiply one input by the stage’s fixed factor, called a twiddle. Add that product to the other input for one output and subtract it for the other. For example, with inputs 1 and 5, a twiddle of 13 and modulus 17, the shared product is 14 after reduction. The outputs are 15 and 4. Try stage 1, pair 1 below.
