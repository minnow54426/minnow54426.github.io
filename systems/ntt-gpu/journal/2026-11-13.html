Compare GPU rentals for the first native session¶

Primary-source rental survey with dated rates, a saved Vast.ai sample, session budgets and profiling constraints.

Outcome¶

Completed a GPU rental market survey for this project's first native CUDA session. Compare RunPod Community and verified Vast.ai RTX 5090 offers; retain RTX 4090 as the control. No GPU was rented or tested. D11 remains open and no roadmap gate advances.

Reasoning¶

The cheapest advertised GPU-hour need not be the cheapest usable experiment. Driver/toolkit compatibility, build resources, selected disk, network charges, setup time and Nsight counter permissions can change the decision. Offline preparation makes a short on-demand session more practical than continuous rental or an early reservation.

Changes¶

  • Added an English comparison of RunPod, Vast.ai, TensorDock, AutoDL, Jarvislabs and Lambda, with clickable official sources and quote limitations.
  • Inspected RunPod's Community and Secure pricing tabs in Chromium and distinguished Lambda's November 16 future-effective prices from current rates.
  • Saved a timestamped, read-only Vast offer sample with its query, compute prices and selected public metadata; omitted public host IP addresses.
  • Added worked compute budgets, stopped-storage billing rules, profiling requirements and a first-session handoff; linked the survey from hardware selection and offline preparation and registered it in the Research sidebar.

Verification¶

Official pricing, billing and NVIDIA permission sources were reviewed on November 13, 2026. The Vast query returned 64 records: 33 RTX 5090 and 31 RTX 4090 offers. Its response reported no truncation; the survey describes a sample, not an exhaustive inventory or guaranteed booking. AutoDL's current GPU offer and all specific hosts' hardware-counter permissions remain unverified.

All 23 documentation unit tests and 14 Node interaction/math tests passed. The build validated local links and fragments for 30 HTML pages, including nine journal entries. Chromium passed 120 page/width checks at 1440/1024/375/320px: English content, rendered mathematics, no page-wide overflow and no script or HTTP errors. The new article and journal passed computed text-contrast checks; section navigation and search worked. Desktop and phone screenshots were inspected, and wide price tables were adjusted for horizontal scrolling on phones.

All 64 saved offer records were checked against every stated query filter; the article's counts, minima, maxima and medians match the snapshot, and each record's compute plus default storage equals its reported total. This research establishes no GPU correctness or performance result. Website export, deployment and live verification follow the source commit.

Open questions¶

Which suitable host is available at checkout? Does its complete configuration provide the required driver, toolkit, build resources and Nsight permissions? What are the actual setup time, verified workload time and final invoice?

Next step¶

Select a live full-GPU on-demand RTX 5090 offer, resolve profiler permissions, then run the prepared session and save evidence. Complete D11 only after observing the real hardware environment and required native checks. Analyze results offline between paid sessions.
