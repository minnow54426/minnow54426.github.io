5. What the GPU tests showed

November 24 · RTX 4080 SUPER. Both CUDA implementations matched the pinned OpenFHE reference in the native tests and passed Compute Sanitizer. The timing sweep used 4,096 and 65,536 coefficients, 3 and 40 prime towers, and 30- and 60-bit primes.

A faster GPU step¶

The group-twiddle implementation sped up forward and inverse NTT on the GPU by a median 1.46× and 1.47× across eight workloads. The full call improved in three of eight workloads for each direction. That call also includes work around the transform, such as moving data.

In the largest 60-bit conversion case, updating a polynomial’s prime towers took 4.24 ms with inputs already on the GPU. Bringing the data in from the CPU and returning the result took 20.99 ms. Copying the intermediate back to the CPU and up again raised that to 39.37 ms.

What comes next¶

Nsight Systems captured GPU activity. The rental blocked Nsight Compute’s hardware counters. The next run will repeat the large 30- and 60-bit cases in balanced order on the target RTX 5090 and trace the 40-tower conversion workload to see where the time goes.
